How it works

One tap a day. The family is told only when something is off.

1

Your parent taps "I am OK"

One big button, once a day, by a time you choose together. No email, no password: they join with a 6-digit code from you.

2

You see it on your phone

The Today screen shows both parents at a glance. A quiet green tick means there is nothing you need to do.

3

If the tap does not come

The app reminds your parent first. Then it tells you, and keeps telling the family until someone answers.

What happens after a missed check-in

After 30 minutes

A gentle reminder to your parent.

After 1 hour

A second reminder to your parent, and a notification to the first family member.

Every 30 minutes

One more family member is told each time.

After 3 hours

An urgent alert to everyone in the family.

Pill reminders

The right pills at the right time, and you know they were taken

  • You set it up once. Add each pill with its dose and time. Morning and evening pills come as one reminder each.
  • A photo of the box. Your parent sees the box they know, not just a name.
  • One tick per pill. They tick what they took and tap "I took all", or only some.
  • You see what was missed. The Pills screen shows each parent's day: taken, and not taken by name.

Part of the Family plan. The app reminds; it does not give medical advice.

Questions

Does my parent need an email or a password?

No. You set up the family, and your parent joins by typing a 6-digit code from you.

What can the family see?

Check-ins, pill reminders and answers. Not location, not messages, not the phone's photo library. Your parent can see exactly what you see, and pause alerts.

What happens if my parent forgets to tap?

After 30 minutes the app reminds them. After an hour it reminds them again and tells the first family member, then one more family member every 30 minutes, and after 3 hours it sends an urgent alert to everyone in the family.

How much does it cost?

The daily check-in is free forever. The Family plan is $59.99 a year or $6.99 a month, with 14 days free, and you can cancel anytime.

Is this an emergency service?

No. It tells the family, not the emergency services. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Family, then Account, then Delete account. Your information is deleted straight away.

How do I reach you?

Write to support@ilovemyparents.app. We answer within two working days.
