I Love My Parents

Know Mom and Dad are OK, every day

One tap from them each morning. Peace of mind for you. If the tap does not come, the family hears about it, gently at first and then urgently.

What the app does

  • A big "I am OK" button for your parent, once a day.
  • "Are you OK?" from the family, answered with two taps on a heart.
  • Pill reminders with a photo of the box.
  • "I need help" tells the whole family at once.

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play. Not an emergency service: in an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Support

Questions, or help with your account or subscription? Write to support@ilovemyparents.app. We answer within two working days.

To delete your account and all its information, open the app and go to Family > Account > Delete account, or write to us.
